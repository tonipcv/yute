What we check, and why it works

  • Domain age & DNS - a "logistics company" whose domain was registered 12 days ago is a conversation starter, not a buyer.
  • Mail servers - real companies receive email. Domains without working MX are a classic mask for cold-contact fronts.
  • Registry & press traces - incorporation mentions, business directories, news. Shells leave gaps where real companies leave records.
  • People behind the domain - links the business to actual decision-makers; person and company checks reinforce each other.
  • Evidence with every signal - auditors see the sources, so false positives are a review, not a dead end.

What is KYB?

Know Your Business: verifying that a business signing up to your platform is real, active, and who it belongs to - the B2B counterpart of KYC.

Does yute replace registry lookups?

No - and you may still need local registry filings for regulated activity. yute is the fast layer in front: it filters obvious fakes at signup so manual or document-based review happens only where it matters.

How fast does yute KYB run?

One API call returns structured signals with sources in under a second - against days per company for document-based processes.
