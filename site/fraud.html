Signals you get, and why they catch fraud

  • Email legitimacy - disposable, free vs corporate, SMTP deliverability. Bounce-heavy lists and throwaway signups die here.
  • Domain age & MX - the "company" someone claims was registered three weeks ago with no mail servers? Flagged.
  • Identity match - does maria.silva@acmecorp.com look like the Maria Silva who exists on the professional web?
  • Web presence - live search across profiles, personal sites, and press. Real people leave trails; fabricated identities don't.
  • Evidence included - every signal comes with the sources behind it, so your trust team can audit, not guess.
