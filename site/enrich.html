How is yute enrich different from Clearbit or Clay?

Provider databases sell the same snapshot to thousands of companies - your CRM gets the same worn rows as everyone else's. yute resolves each query with live web search at request time, and you pay per lookup instead of a monthly subscription.

What does enrichment cost?

One credit per resolved lookup. Misses are free - you never pay for a person yute could not find. The first 100 lookups are free.

Can I enrich a whole CRM list?

Yes. POST /v1/enrich uploads the list as an async job and yute webhooks the enriched rows as they resolve.
