What the verdict covers

  • Syntax and MX - that the domain exists and is pointed at a mail server worth talking to.
  • SMTP handshake - the same RCPT conversation your campaign would have, so invalid mailboxes are caught at validation time, not bounce time.
  • Catch-all detection - domains that accept everything to avoid detection; yute flags their risk instead of vouching for them.
  • Disposable providers - the mailbox that works right now and dies by Friday.
  • Deliverability verdict - one clear signal per address, with the evidence behind it for auditors and post-mortems.

Why does email validation matter for deliverability?

Mail providers watch your bounce rate. Invalid addresses that bounce push your domain toward the spam folder for everyone - even valid recipients. Validating before send keeps the bounce rate low and sender reputation intact.

How is SMTP validation different from syntax checks?

Syntax and DNS checks only confirm an address looks valid. yute talks to the recipient's mail server the same way your campaign would and asks if the mailbox exists - catching invalid addresses, and catch-all domains that syntax checks wave through.
